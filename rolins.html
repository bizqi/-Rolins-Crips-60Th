ASAL USUL

Amidst the chaos caused by the pandemic in Las Venturas, where everyone was lost and the economy was collapsing, a group of street youths from the Old Venturas Strip formed a gang called ROLIN'S CRIPS 60TH, led by the calm but sharp Mateo, with his second-in-command, Endang, a brutal but loyal figure. Starting out hanging out in the back alleys of closed casinos, they began to unite hopeless street kids, forming strong bonds, marking their territory with blue, and maintaining strict discipline. This gang is not only a symbol of rebellion, but also resistance to social injustice during the crisis. The name ROLIN'S CRIPS 60TH slowly echoed throughout Las Venturas as one of the most respected gangs during the pandemic.
